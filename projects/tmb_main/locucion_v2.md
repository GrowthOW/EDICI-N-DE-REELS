# TOUR DU MONT BLANC — Locución v2 (para grabar)

Versión recortada del guion v1: mismas ideas y mismo orden, sin
repeticiones, con los datos nuevos (7–12 días, 12–20 km/día según etapa) y
cierre con la web. Idioma: inglés.

Ritmo previsto: pausado, documental (~145 palabras/min). `/` = pausa corta,
`//` = pausa larga (cambio de bloque).

---

### 1 · HOOK
*(2–3 s de sonido ambiente antes de empezar)*

Some mountains are meant to be admired. / Others are meant to be explored. //
And the Tour du Mont Blanc is one of the most extraordinary ways to experience the Alps on foot. /
A loop around the Mont Blanc massif, through France, Italy and Switzerland.

### 2 · WHAT IS THE TMB *(mapa)*

The full loop is around 174 kilometres, and most itineraries take between seven and twelve days, /
starting in Les Houches and finishing in Chamonix. //
And the landscape never stays the same: /
alpine valleys, high mountain passes, forests, glaciers, /
and some of the most iconic villages in the Alps.

### 3 · THE JOURNEY *(el mapa sigue la ruta)*

From Les Houches, the trail heads south through Les Contamines and Les Chapieux, /
then crosses into Italy — to Courmayeur and the spectacular Val Ferret. /
The Grand Col Ferret leads into Switzerland, past La Fouly, Champex and Trient, /
before the trail crosses back into France and returns to the Chamonix Valley.

### 4 · WHY IS IT SO SPECIAL?

But the Tour du Mont Blanc isn't really about the kilometres. /
It's about what happens in between. //
Walking beneath glaciers. / Crossing high mountain passes. / Descending into small Alpine villages. /
Stepping from one country into the next, on foot — /
and watching the landscape, the food and the culture change as you go.

### 5 · WHAT IS A DAY LIKE?

Most days start with breakfast and a new stage ahead. /
Depending on the stage, you'll walk between twelve and twenty kilometres. /
But in the Alps, distance is only part of the story: /
expect big climbs and descents, day after day. //
It's a journey for confident mountain walkers, / ready for a real adventure.

### 6 · YOU DON'T HAVE TO RUSH IT

And it doesn't need to be rushed. /
There's time to enjoy places like Courmayeur and Chamonix. /
Because the Tour isn't only about reaching the end.

### 7 · HOW ORBIS WAYS MAKES IT POSSIBLE

Of course, organising a journey through three countries isn't simple. /
That's where Orbis Ways comes in. //
We organise your accommodation along the route, /
with breakfast every day and selected dinners. /
We transfer your luggage wherever the itinerary allows, /
so most days you walk with just a small daypack. /
Plus detailed route information and maps for every stage, /
and 24-hour telephone assistance.

### 8 · THE ORBIS IDEA

We take care of everything behind the journey. //
So each morning, you can focus on something much simpler. //
Walking.

### 9 · CLOSING

Seven to twelve days. / Three countries. /
And 174 kilometres through some of the most spectacular landscapes in the Alps. //
There are many ways to experience Mont Blanc. /
Walking around it might just be the most unforgettable.
