# TOUR DU MONT BLANC — Orbis Ways, video principal

Guion recibido del cliente (versión 1). Duración objetivo: 2:30–3:00.
Notas de revisión al final.

---

## 00:00–00:15 — THE HOOK

**VISUAL:** Wide cinematic shots of the Mont Blanc massif. Glaciers. Mountain
trails. Hikers moving through the landscape. Natural sound for the first few
seconds.

**VO:** Some mountains are meant to be admired. / Others are meant to be
explored. / And the Tour du Mont Blanc is one of the most extraordinary ways
to experience the Alps on foot. / A journey around the Mont Blanc massif,
crossing three countries along the way.

**ON SCREEN:** TOUR DU MONT BLANC · FRANCE · ITALY · SWITZERLAND

## 00:15–00:38 — WHAT IS THE TOUR DU MONT BLANC?

**VISUAL:** Animated map. Mont Blanc appears in the centre. A line begins in
Les Houches and gradually travels around the massif.

**VO:** The Tour du Mont Blanc is a long-distance hiking route that circles
the Mont Blanc massif through France, Italy and Switzerland. / Orbis Ways'
complete itinerary covers approximately 174 kilometres, starting in Les
Houches and finishing in Chamonix. / Along the way, the landscape changes
constantly. / Alpine valleys, high mountain passes, forests, glaciers and
some of the most iconic villages in the Alps.

**ON SCREEN:** 174 KM · 3 COUNTRIES · 14 DAYS

## 00:38–01:05 — THE JOURNEY

**VISUAL:** Continue travelling around the animated map. Combine each location
with real footage: LES HOUCHES → LES CONTAMINES → LES CHAPIEUX → COURMAYEUR →
VAL FERRET → LA FOULY → CHAMPEX → TRIENT → CHAMONIX

**VO:** The journey begins in the Chamonix Valley, in Les Houches. / From
here, the trail heads towards Les Contamines and Les Chapieux before crossing
the mountains into Italy. / Then come Courmayeur and the spectacular Val
Ferret. / From Italy, you cross the Grand Col Ferret into Switzerland,
continuing through places such as La Fouly, Champex and Trient. / And
eventually, the trail crosses back into France and returns to the Chamonix
Valley. / A complete journey around one of Europe's most famous mountain
ranges.

## 01:05–01:28 — WHY IS IT SO SPECIAL?

**VISUAL:** Slow the edit down. Glacier. Alpine meadow. Mountain pass. Small
village. Mountain café. Boots on the trail. Hikers stopping to look at the
landscape.

**VO:** But the Tour du Mont Blanc isn't just about covering 174 kilometres.
/ It's about everything you experience along the way. / Walking beneath
glaciers. / Crossing high mountain passes. / Descending into small Alpine
villages. / Crossing from one country into another on foot. / And watching
the landscape, the food and the culture change as you make your way around
the mountain.

## 01:28–01:52 — WHAT IS A DAY LIKE?

**VISUAL:** Morning at the hotel. Breakfast. Hiker prepares a small daypack.
Leaves the hotel. Trail. Lunch stop. Walking. Arrival at the next village.

**VO:** Most days begin with breakfast and a new section of trail ahead of
you. / Distances vary from stage to stage, but many days involve around 12
to 20 kilometres of walking. / And in the Alps, distance is only part of the
challenge. / There are significant climbs and descents, changing terrain and
consecutive days on the trail. / So this is a journey for people who are
comfortable hiking in the mountains and are prepared for a real walking
adventure.

**ON SCREEN:** 12–20 KM / DAY · *Distances vary by stage*

## 01:52–02:08 — YOU DON'T HAVE TO RUSH IT

**VISUAL:** Courmayeur. Coffee on a terrace. Hikers enjoying the village.
Cable car / mountain scenery. Then Chamonix.

**VO:** And a journey like this doesn't need to be rushed. / The complete
Orbis Ways itinerary takes 14 days and includes time to enjoy places such as
Courmayeur and Chamonix, as well as some additional walks around the Mont
Blanc massif. / Because the Tour isn't only about reaching the end. / It's
about experiencing the Alps along the way.

## 02:08–02:42 — HOW ORBIS WAYS MAKES IT POSSIBLE

**VISUAL:** Transition from destination footage to real service footage.
Hotel exterior. Reception. Room. Breakfast. Suitcase being labelled. Suitcase
loaded into vehicle. Route information / maps. Member of Orbis team providing
assistance. Hikers leaving with small daypacks.

**VO:** Of course, organising a journey through three countries can be
complicated. / That's where Orbis Ways comes in. / We organise your
accommodation along the route. / Breakfast is included throughout the trip,
with selected dinners also arranged along the way. / We transfer your luggage
between accommodations wherever the itinerary allows, so most days you can
walk with just a small daypack. / You'll also receive detailed route
information and maps to help you follow each stage. / And if something
unexpected happens during your trip, you'll have access to 24-hour telephone
assistance.

**ON SCREEN:** ACCOMMODATION · LUGGAGE TRANSFERS* · BREAKFAST + SELECTED
DINNERS · ROUTE INFORMATION & MAPS · 24/7 TELEPHONE ASSISTANCE ·
*According to itinerary

## 02:42–02:57 — THE ORBIS IDEA

**VISUAL:** Close shot: large suitcase is placed into the luggage transfer
vehicle. CUT. The same travellers put on their small backpacks and start
walking. Then a wide landscape shot.

**VO:** We take care of the logistics behind the journey. / The
accommodation. / The luggage. / The route information. / And the support
along the way. / So that each morning, you can focus on something much
simpler. / Walking.

## 02:57–03:15 — CLOSING

**VISUAL:** Golden-hour footage. Two hikers walking away from camera. Mont
Blanc massif in the distance. Natural, calm, aspirational.

**VO:** Fourteen days. / Three countries. / And 174 kilometres through some
of the most spectacular landscapes in the Alps. / There are many ways to
experience Mont Blanc. / Walking around it might just be one of the most
unforgettable.

**ON SCREEN:** TOUR DU MONT BLANC · The journey is yours. We take care of the
rest. · ORBIS WAYS
